# Source notice

The gong strikes and reference performances in `public/audio/` come from the
[Katunog](https://katunog.asti.dost.gov.ph/) record `PIISD02606`, Kulintang
(Maranao), recorded in Tugaya, Lanao del Sur and performed by Caironesa C.
Dimatanday. They are included under the project operator's stated permission
for this educational, non-commercial work.

The selected MP3 audio streams are copied without their embedded cover images
to keep the web player small. Filenames in this repository are shortened, but
the original note and piece names remain documented here and in the interface.
The simulator includes all 48 standard `P1` strikes for notes `N1` through
`N8`; the unidentified `N1_P2` articulation is not used. The gong controls use
relative numbers because kulintang sets do not share one standard Western
tuning.

The Lapa, Epping Forest 01, and Park Music Stage HDR environments and resized
tonemapped WebP panoramas are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) and are
available under CC0. They are used as settings, not presented as documentary
images of Lanao del Sur.

The MIT License in `LICENSE` applies only to the original simulator code. It
does not relicense the Katunog recordings. The Poly Haven files retain their
CC0 status.
